# Design research — v5, the shell

Written 2026-09-11, before the v5 boards, per `SPEC.md` → "# v5 — Simpler" → Design process and `goals/v5.md` Chunk A item 1. Every surface below was opened in the browser pane and measured in the live DOM, not recalled; two are marked as only partly readable. The v4 note (`design/research.md`) covered *what a film app should look like* and produced board C. This note covers one narrower question: **what a chat app's shell looks like** — sidebar, sessions, composer, message rows, empty state — because Jake's verdict on v4 was that the chat "looks terrible" and that ChatGPT / Claude / Cursor had it right.

The brief behind the looking: Headshot v5 is *a log of everything you've watched that can talk*, with exactly three places — Log, Ask, You. Ask has to read as a real chat. The sidebar has to hold three **sections** and, under Ask, a list of **sessions**, and the two must never be confused (Jake, on Glox: "sessions of chats are different than sidebar options"). Board C's tokens and type do not change: Geist + JetBrains Mono, `#0d0d0e` ground, one accent (`#f0b429`) doing one job (Play), no bordered cards, no stat tiles, no uppercase tracked labels.

---

## 1. chatgpt.com — logged out (the real shell, not a marketing page)

The logged-out landing is the product: the same sidebar and composer a signed-in user gets, with the session list simply absent. Dismissed the "Welcome back" account chooser to see it; did not sign in.

- **Sidebar.** 260px, `#000` — the *same* ground as the page, no fill, no border-right beyond a near-invisible hairline. It is one flat column: a 52px icon strip at the very top (logo, search, collapse), then the section rows — `New chat`, `Images`, `Plugins`, `Deep research` — each 36px tall, 16px text, a 20px monoline icon at 12px inset, the active row carrying a subtle `#212121` fill at 8px radius. Then **nothing**: a large empty middle where the session list lives when you are signed in. Then, pinned to the bottom, the quiet utilities (`See plans and pricing`, `Settings`, `Help`) in the same row shape, then the account block.
- **Sections vs sessions.** They are separated by *position and emptiness*, not by decoration: sections at the top, sessions in the scrolling middle, account at the bottom. Signed in, sessions are the same 36px row minus the icon, grouped under small dim date headers.
- **Composer.** 640px wide, 52px tall, 28px radius (a full pill), `#212121` fill, no border. A `+` at the left, placeholder `Ask anything` at 16px, two mic/voice glyphs at the right. No send button until you type. It is *centered in the content column*, not pinned to its bottom edge, when the thread is empty.
- **Message rows.** Not observable logged out (see Unreadable).
- **Empty state.** One line of 16px regular sans, centered, sitting ~40px above the composer, and it *rotates* — "What's on the agenda today?" on one load, "Ready when you are." on the next. No cards, no suggestion chips, no examples grid. `chat.openai.com` 301s to `chatgpt.com`; same page.
- **Take:** the sidebar as *one flat column on the same ground as the page*, with sections top / sessions middle / account bottom and no boxes anywhere. And the empty state as **one sentence above the composer** — that is exactly the shape of Headshot's tonight opener. Also: the composer is the only filled shape on an empty screen, which is the discipline board C already has.
- **Avoid:** the 28px pill radius and the 16px type — at board C's density that composer would read as a toy. Headshot's composer keeps its hairline-and-`>`-prompt grammar. Also avoid the icon strip above the wordmark; Headshot has one wordmark and no collapse.

## 2. claude.ai — signed in (Jake's own session was already open in the pane)

The best available reference, because it is the only one where the **session list under sections** was visible live. Measured in the DOM; Jake's own chat titles are his data and are not reproduced here beyond noting their shape, and no thread was opened.

- **Sidebar.** 288px, ground `#111111` — one step **darker** than the page's `#151515`. A serif-ish `Claude` wordmark top-left with a collapse and a search glyph to its right. Under it a two-up segmented toggle (`Home` / `Code`).
- **Section list.** `New` (emphasised, carrying a fill), then `Projects`, `Artifacts`, `Scheduled`, `Customize`. Every row is **32px tall, 272px wide at an 8px inset, 8px radius, 14px text, a 20px monoline icon, ink at `#c3c2b7`**.
- **Sessions.** Two grouped lists below the sections — `Projects` then `Chats and tasks` — each introduced by a **13px dim (`#898781`) sentence-case group header at a 14px inset**, with a small affordance pushed right (`+` on Projects, a filter glyph on Chats). The session rows are **the identical 32px / 8px-radius row** as the sections, minus the icon; a tiny dot sits where the icon was. Titles are single-line and truncate at the sidebar edge with no ellipsis flourish.
- **The separation is the finding.** Sections and sessions are *the same row*. What separates them is (a) a dim group header naming the list, (b) order, and (c) the section rows carrying icons where session rows carry a near-invisible dot. No divider rule, no ground shift, no size change.
- **Footer.** Pinned bottom: a `Design` row (still a section), then a hairline, then the account row — 24px avatar, `jake · Max`, a chevron that opens the menu, and one utility glyph at the right.
- **Composer, empty state.** Centered content column (685px available; the composer takes ~557px of it), composer 20px radius on `#20201f`, **two-tier and ~98px tall**: the text row on top (`How can I help you today?`), a control row underneath carrying `+`, a `Chat` / `Cowork` mode toggle at the left and the model name plus mic/voice at the right. Above it, one centered greeting with a small brand mark: *"Back at it, jake"* — first-person, lowercase name, no punctuation ceremony.
- **Take:** three things, almost verbatim. **(a)** the dim sentence-case group header as the only thing dividing sections from sessions — it is cheaper than a rule and it obeys "no uppercase tracked labels" already. **(b)** one row shape for both, with an icon on a section and nothing on a session. **(c)** the account row pinned at the bottom, name + a menu, which is exactly SPEC's footer.
- **Avoid:** the darker sidebar ground (board C's rail is the same `#0d0d0e` as the page, separated by one hairline — do not introduce a second ground), the 8px-radius filled row (board C's active state is *ink level*, not a pill), the segmented `Home`/`Code` toggle, and the two-tier composer's control row full of model/mode machinery. Headshot has one model and no modes; that row would be empty theatre.

## 3. grok.com — logged out

- **Sidebar.** There is none. Logged out, Grok is a bare top bar (logo left; `Imagine`, a settings glyph, `Sign in`, `Sign up` right) over an empty field.
- **Composer.** ~750px wide, ~100px tall, **two-tier** like Claude's: placeholder line on top (`Switch to Build Mode to create apps`), a control row under it with `+` at the left and a model-speed selector (`Fast ⌄`) plus a filled circular send button at the right. Radius ~24px on a fill barely lighter than the page.
- **Empty state.** One centered headline, ~24px medium — *"What should we explore?"* — with a hand-drawn underline flourish under the last two words, sitting ~40px above the composer. The composer sits at roughly one third of the viewport height, not at the bottom.
- **Ground.** `rgb(19,18,17)` — a genuinely *warm* near-black, within a hair of board C's `#0d0d0e`. Worth recording as independent confirmation that the warm black is the current house colour for this category and that a blue-black would date the product.
- **Take:** the warm ground (confirmation only), and the proportion — headline, ~40px of air, composer, and the whole group sitting high in the column rather than centered.
- **Avoid:** hiding the session list entirely when signed out. Headshot signed-out is Ask-only, and SPEC already says the sidebar shows Ask and a Sign-in row — a shell that vanishes is worse than a shell with one item. Also avoid the circular send button; board C's composer ends in a mono `⏎`, and a send button would be the second filled shape on the screen.

## 4. cursor.com — the product pages (the chat panel as shown in the hero)

The hero is an animated Cursor Desktop window, which is the closest thing available to Cursor's real chat surface without installing it.

- **Sidebar.** ~180px of the window, and it holds **only sessions** — no section nav at all. They are grouped by *state* under small uppercase-tracked headers with a count: `IN PROGRESS 2`, `READY FOR REVIEW 3`. Each session is a two-line row: a status glyph at the left, the title at ~13px in ink, a dim second line beneath it saying what it is doing (`Reading docs`, `Generating plan`, `Done. Fonts preload in the he…`), and a right-aligned dim age (`now`, `10m`, `30m`, `45m`). One row carries a diff readout instead (`+135 −21`).
- **Message rows.** The centre column is the chat, ~250px wide in the mock: the user's message sits in a **filled, hairline-bordered box at the top**, full column width, left-aligned; the agent's output below it is **plain left-aligned text with no bubble and no avatar**, interleaved with small file rows (`app/page.tsx +62`). Nothing is right-aligned; nobody has an avatar.
- **Composer.** Not visible in the captured frames of the animation.
- **Changelog.** Ground `rgb(20,18,11)` — a brown-black. Column 768px. Each entry is `Sep 10, 2026 · Changelog` in 16px dim, then the title at **36px / 400 / −0.02em** in CursorGothic, then body at 16/24 at 60% ink, then `h2` subheads at 22/700. No card, no border, no badge, no version chip. The current top entry, *Cursor Projects*, is itself the relevant datum: it says "Access Projects from the left-hand nav" — Cursor is adding exactly the sections-above-sessions nav that Glox already has.
- **Take:** the **two-line session row with a right-aligned dim age** (title on top, the thing itself underneath), and the asymmetry of the message rows — user in a contained shape, assistant as plain full-width text. That asymmetry is what makes a thread scan as a thread without avatars.
- **Avoid:** the uppercase-tracked group headers (forbidden in board C — Claude's dim sentence-case header does the same job), the status glyph per session (Headshot's sessions have no state), and the two-line row at Headshot's sidebar width: 232px with a 13px title and a 11.5px subtitle would be a wall. One line per session.

## 5. ryo.lu — revisited for the Cursor chat

Opened again on the strength of §6 of the v4 note. It has changed only in its status line (`LAST SEEN 18 HOURS AGO, AT DIN TAI FUNG (鼎泰豐) IN TAIPEI.`) and gained a small floating ryOS desktop widget in the top-right corner. **It does not show a Cursor chat panel** — the page is still the huge personal sentence, the quiet mono status line, and two low columns of tiny underlined mono links. Nothing new for the shell; what it taught the v4 note (the sentence is the page, the machinery is small and dim beneath it) is already in board C and in DESIGN.md's copy rules.

- **Take:** nothing new. It does re-confirm the one rule the tonight opener depends on: a single first-person sentence set at reading size, with the metadata demoted to a small dim line under it, is enough to carry a screen.
- **Avoid:** as before, the near-empty screen.

## 6. Glox Brain Lab — the sidebar organisation, read from source

Read from `~/Desktop/glox-brain-lab/src/components/app-shell.tsx` and `app-shell.module.css`, not from a screenshot. This is the organisation Jake pointed at; the visual design is explicitly not being copied.

- **Shape.** `grid-template-columns: 232px minmax(0, 1fr)`. The sidebar is `position: sticky`, full viewport height, `padding: 22px 16px 18px`, and a flex column.
- **Brand.** A 32px rounded mark plus a two-line lockup (`Glox` bold over a dim `Company Brain`), `padding: 4px 7px 22px` — i.e. the wordmark owns a 22px gap below it before the nav starts.
- **Sections.** A flat `display: grid; gap: 4px` list of four: `Today`, `Ask`, `Company`, `Feedback`. Each is **44px min-height, 11px gap between a 19px icon and its label, 12px horizontal padding, 8px radius, label 0.86rem / 560 weight**. Inactive `#a5a5ad`; hover `#fff` on `#1a1a1d`; active `#fff` on `#202024` with the icon tinted. That is the whole nav.
- **Footer.** `margin-top: auto`, a 1px top rule, a 7px status dot and one small dim line (`Brain available`) at 0.72rem. Nothing else is pinned down there.
- **Sessions.** There are none in this file — and that is the point Jake was making. Glox's sidebar holds *sections only*, at a fixed count, with the chat's own sessions living elsewhere in the Ask surface. The organisation he likes is: **a short, fixed, icon-led section list at the top; a quiet footer; and sessions kept out of that list.**
- **Phone.** Under 760px the grid collapses to a block, the sidebar becomes a sticky top strip, and the nav becomes `grid-template-columns: repeat(4, 1fr)` of 48px items — icon over label, 0.72rem, and under 420px the item stacks icon-over-label at 0.66rem. The brand subtitle and the footer are `display: none`. That is a bottom-tab-bar layout drawn at the top; Headshot puts it at the bottom, where a thumb is.
- **Take:** the **232px width** (SPEC already fixed it), the fixed short section list with a small icon each, the `margin-top: auto` footer, and — the actual point — **sessions do not live in the section list**. In Headshot they live *under* the Ask section, indented, which keeps the promise while still putting the list one click from where you are.
- **Avoid:** the whole visual layer — the blue brand mark, the filled 8px-radius active pill, the two-line brand lockup, the green status dot, the 44px nav rows. Board C's active state is ink level, its rail is the page's own ground with one hairline, and it has no status dot. Also avoid Glox's sidebar-as-top-strip on phone; Headshot's tab bar goes to the bottom.

## Synthesis — the exact shell decisions for Headshot

Three patterns hold across all four products, and each of them is cheaper than what v4 built. **First, the shell is one ground.** ChatGPT's sidebar is the same black as its page; Cursor's panel is the same brown-black as its editor. Only Claude shifts, by one step, and Headshot does not need to — board C separates the rail with a single hairline and that is the house rule. **Second, sections and sessions are the same row, told apart by a dim sentence-case group header and by order, not by shape, rule, colour or fill.** Claude does this explicitly; ChatGPT does it by leaving a hole; Cursor does it with uppercase headers Headshot may not use. **Third, the empty state is one sentence above a large composer, and the composer is the only filled thing on the screen.** All three put a single line of first-person copy where a dashboard would go — which is precisely what Headshot's tonight opener is, and why it belongs in Ask and not on You.

The decisions, in board C's tokens (`DESIGN.md`), for the two v5 boards:

1. **Sidebar width 232px** (SPEC/Glox), replacing board C's 176px rail. Ground `--bg` `#0d0d0e` — the same as the page — with `border-right: 1px var(--line)`. Padding `22px 14px 18px`, so rows can carry a 12px inset and still sit on a 26px gutter.
2. **Wordmark** `headshot`, Geist 14 / 700 / −0.02em at `--ink`, with 22px of air under it (Glox's brand gap), and no icon strip, no collapse control, no subtitle.
3. **Section list: exactly three** — `log`, `ask`, `you` — lowercase, in that order, each a **32px row** (Claude's height, not Glox's 44px, because board C's scale is denser) with a **16px monoline stroke icon** at `--ink-faint` and a `.t-ui` label (Geist 13.5 / 500). Active is `--ink` with the icon at `--ink`; inactive is `--ink-faint`. **No pill, no fill, no active bar** — the ink level is the state, per DESIGN.md § Sidebar. Hover is `--row-hover` at a 4px radius, borrowed from `.row`.
4. **Sessions live under Ask, and only when Ask is active.** Indented to a 28px inset so the indent itself says "these belong to that": first a `new chat` row (Geist 13.5 at `--ink`, with a 14px `+` glyph — ChatGPT's and Claude's emphasised first item, but drawn as a row, not a button), then the session titles as **28px single-line rows**, Geist 13 at `--ink-dim`, the open one at `--ink` 500, truncating at the rail edge. One line each, no timestamp column, no second line — Cursor's two-line row does not survive 232px at this type scale.
5. **One dim group header, and only one.** `chats` in Geist 13 at `--ink-faint`, sentence case, above the session list — Claude's separator, which is already legal under "no uppercase tracked labels". Nothing else divides the sidebar: no rule between sections and sessions.
6. **Account footer** pinned with `margin-top: auto`: a 32px row with `jake@…` in `.t-meta` at `--ink-faint` and a 16px settings glyph pushed right, over one `--line` hairline. That hairline and the rail's own border are the only two lines in the sidebar.
7. **Ask column: 720px, centered** in the content pane (SPEC's figure; it also matches what the references do — a capped centred measure, ~80% of the available pane at a laptop width, rather than the full column). Content padding stays `22px 34px 32px`.
8. **Message rows keep board C's bubbles, and the asymmetry stays**: yours `.bubble-you` on `--bg-raised`, hugging the right edge; the assistant's `.bubble-them` on `--bg-elev`, left, capped at 74%; **no avatars, no name labels, no timestamps per row** — the only per-row metadata is the receipts ledger under an answer. Stack gap 14px. This is the one place the references and board C already agree, and the only change v5 makes is the column they sit in.
9. **Composer: one tier, 56px, pinned to the bottom of the Ask column at the full 720px** — noticeably bigger than v4's 44px inline composer (that is the "big composer" Jake asked for) but still board C's grammar: a mono `>` prompt at `--ink-ghost`, Geist 15 text, a `--line-input` hairline under it rather than a fill, and a mono `⏎` pushed right. **No pill, no send button, no `+`, no model selector, no mode toggle** — Claude's and Grok's control rows exist to hold machinery Headshot does not have, and an empty control row is the definition of chrome.
10. **Empty state = the tonight opener**, sitting where all three references put their greeting: one assistant row, first person, at the top of an otherwise empty 720px column — *"tonight: **The Dark Knight Rises** on HBO Max, because it makes it 8 for **Christopher Nolan** after **The Dark Knight** and **Interstellar**"* — with a single `.btn-play` beside it and a `.t-meta` line under it. That Play is the one accent in the whole view, which is why the Log board's Catch-up control is `.btn-quiet` and not amber.

One thing the references do *not* settle, and the Log board decides on its own: the row of ten. Nobody in this category rates anything, so it comes from IMDb's own control as Jake asked — ten 36px cells in a single 400px row, mono figures at `--ink-faint`, the chosen one `.btn-solid` (ink fill, ground label), keyboard hints `1…0` in `.t-meta` beside it. Words become numbers; nothing else about the log field changes.
